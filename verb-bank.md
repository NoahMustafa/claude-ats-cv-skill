# Action Verb Bank

Use these past-tense verbs to open CV bullets. Never repeat the same verb twice within
a single role. Rotate across categories based on what the bullet is describing.

## Data & Analysis
Analysed, Processed, Extracted, Transformed, Modelled, Forecasted, Evaluated, Mapped,
Queried, Mined, Aggregated, Segmented, Validated, Cleaned, Interpreted, Visualised,
Benchmarked, Profiled, Monitored, Tracked, Computed, Calculated, Classified, Detected

## Engineering & Development
Built, Developed, Designed, Engineered, Implemented, Deployed, Constructed, Architected,
Refactored, Automated, Integrated, Migrated, Configured, Optimised, Streamlined,
Containerised, Packaged, Compiled, Debugged, Maintained, Upgraded, Extended, Modularised

## Impact & Delivery
Reduced, Increased, Improved, Accelerated, Delivered, Achieved, Generated, Enabled,
Resolved, Eliminated, Cut, Scaled, Expanded, Consolidated, Saved, Boosted, Lowered,
Accelerated, Shortened, Simplified, Standardised

## Leadership & Collaboration (use sparingly — only if genuinely applicable)
Coordinated, Led, Managed, Mentored, Trained, Presented, Collaborated, Facilitated,
Guided, Advised, Onboarded, Reviewed, Documented, Reported, Communicated

## Research & Innovation
Investigated, Researched, Prototyped, Tested, Experimented, Benchmarked, Piloted,
Hypothesised, Simulated, Calibrated, Tuned, Optimised, Iterated

---

## Usage Rules for the Agent

1. Never start two bullets in the same role with the same verb.
2. Prefer verbs from the "Impact & Delivery" category when a metric or result follows.
3. Prefer verbs from "Engineering & Development" when describing what was built.
4. Prefer verbs from "Data & Analysis" for data pipeline and analytics bullets.
5. Avoid ALL verbs from "Leadership & Collaboration" unless the user explicitly described
   a leadership or collaboration responsibility. Do not assume.
6. Never use: "leveraged", "spearheaded", "utilized", "facilitated" (unless genuinely
   about facilitation), "championed", "evangelized", "owned" (in the startup sense).
